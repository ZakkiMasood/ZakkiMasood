## What I work on

I build models that have to produce a decision before the data is complete.

Household demand forecasting. Pump scheduling against live market prices. Battery
state-of-charge prediction for grid frequency regulation. Power allocation across
distributed-antenna IoT networks. The application changes; the structure does not —
a real system, a hard optimization underneath it, and irreducible uncertainty in between.

Six peer-reviewed publications in IEEE, ACM and Elsevier venues. Over three years as a
Research Fellow at Singapore University of Technology and Design. Currently moving this
work from academia into industry.

---

## Focus areas

**Probabilistic forecasting** — quantile methods, calibrated prediction intervals, deep
sequence models (LSTM, Seq2Seq), clustering-based population segmentation

**Optimization** — convex and fractional programming, scheduling under dynamic pricing,
demand-side management, constrained decision-making

**Time series at scale** — multivariate multi-step prediction, backtesting, evaluation
beyond RMSE

**Sensor networks** — energy-efficient power allocation, localization, mobile data
collection, network lifetime
